RTSP Cam View Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Camera connections

RTSP Cam View discovers compatible cameras on your network and displays the live streams you select. The app uses camera addresses, device and stream details, and any camera credentials you provide to connect to those cameras. Discovery requests are sent on the local network. Camera authentication, playback and camera controls communicate with the camera endpoints you select. The network protocol and its encryption depend on the camera and connection.

Data stored on your device

The app stores registered camera details and saved camera credentials in encrypted storage on your device. Your display and playback preferences, trial start, and purchase access status are also stored on your device. Camera streams are processed for live display.

Purchases

Google Play Billing handles purchases. The app uses purchase information returned by Google Play to check and restore access. Google handles payment information under its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

Registered camera details and saved credentials remain on the device until you remove that camera or clear the app's data. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes the app's local settings and records. Google Play manages records of Google Play purchases.
